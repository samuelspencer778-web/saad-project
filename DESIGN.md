## Brand & Style

This design system is tailored for a high-converting pediatric dental landing page designed to turn anxious parents into confident, booked patients. The aesthetic balances holistic organic warmth with clinical credibility. 

The design style unites **Modern Organic Tactility** with **Clean Conversion Architecture**:
- **Organic Earthiness & Calming Warmth:** Earthy forest tones, soothing moss greens, and warm sunlight hues ease parental apprehension and pediatric anxiety, diverging from sterile, cold clinical clinics.
- **Clear Conversion Hierarchy:** Highly intentional use of terracotta orange directs immediate visual focus toward primary conversion pathways (instant online booking, direct calling, and appointment confirmation).
- **Safe & Whimsical Professionalism:** Rounded geometric contours, subtle organic container shapes, friendly badge elements, and unmistakable trust markers (board certifications, verified Google 5-star ratings, anxiety-free sedation guarantees) project deep medical authority while remaining inherently approachable for children.

## Colors

The color palette reflects an earthy canopy with radiant sun accents and an unmistakable conversion trigger:

- **Primary (`#5B6440`):** Forest Olive represents natural sanctuary, stability, and medical integrity. Used for section headers, dominant brand backgrounds, primary trust icons, and grounding visual containers.
- **Secondary (`#9AA277`):** Soft Sage acts as a tranquil bridging hue for secondary banners, badge backgrounds, and non-intrusive container surfaces.
- **Tertiary (`#F7C153`):** Sun Gold symbolizes gentle playfulness, morning warmth, and glowing smiles. Applied to star ratings, highlight ribbons, and delight markers.
- **CTA Highlight (`#D95B1E`):** Terracotta Orange is reserved explicitly for conversion actions: "Book Appointment," emergency dental dialers, and primary lead capture buttons. Its high chromatic contrast against sage and cream draws parental eyes instantly.
- **Neutrals & Surfaces (`#FAF9F6`, `#FFFFFF`, `#222222`):** A soft, warm off-white canvas eliminates clinical glare while maintaining an open, sterile-clean feel. Deep charcoal text guarantees readability without the harsh contrast of pure black.

## Typography

**Plus Jakarta Sans** is the unified typographic foundation across all headlines, body copy, and UI controls. Its friendly geometric curves and open apertures evoke youthful delight without descending into childish informality. 

- **Display & Headlines:** Set in bold and extra-bold weights with slight negative letter spacing to feel modern, confident, and grounded.
- **Body Text:** Renders with generous line heights (1.5x to 1.6x) to ensure effortless readability for parents scanning on mobile devices.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** High-contrast, semi-bold to bold weights support instant recognition for conversion CTAs, trust ratings, and booking disclaimers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column grid for desktop (max-width `1200px`) transitioning into a 4-column fluid layout on mobile.

- **Desktop Rhythm:** Ample vertical breathing room (`space-3xl` between landing page sections) creates a peaceful, unhurried cadence that reassures parents. 
- **Mobile Density:** Sticky bottom-bar actions ("Call Now" + "Book Online") maintain instant reachability. Mobile padding collapses gracefully to `margin-mobile` to preserve screen real estate for direct trust testimonials, insurance logos, and provider bios.
- **Component Padding:** Standard cards leverage `space-lg` (24px) padding internally, with grouped form fields utilizing consistent `space-md` gaps.

## Elevation & Depth

This system intentionally departs from heavy, dramatic shadows in favor of a soft, illuminated tactile depth that mimics natural light filtering through trees:

- **Surface Layers:** The foundational backdrop uses warm off-white (`#FAF9F6`). Primary content cards sit on pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with ultra-fine, organic borders (`#E5E7DC`).
- **Warm Ambient Shadows:** Elevated cards and modal booking panels use warm, tinted box shadows: `0 8px 30px -4px rgba(91, 100, 64, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(91, 100, 64, 0.04)`. This green-tinted diffusion avoids dull gray shadows and keeps the interface glowing and healthy.
- **Interactive Focus & Hover:** Interactive elements subtly elevate with an expanded warm shadow (`0 14px 34px -4px rgba(217, 91, 30, 0.18)` for CTA buttons) accompanied by a -2px vertical translation.

## Shapes

A shape language of **Level 2 (Rounded)** infuses warmth and softness across the entire experience.

- **Containers & Cards:** Feature `1rem` (16px) to `1.5rem` (24px) corner radii (`rounded-lg` and `rounded-xl`), removing clinical sharpness.
- **Badges & Pill Highlights:** Trust badges, child age pills (e.g., "Infants 0–3", "Ages 4–12"), and review score banners adopt fully rounded pill silhouettes (`9999px`) to express playfulness and approachability.
- **Inputs & CTAs:** Interactive touchpoints use `0.75rem` (12px) to `1rem` (16px) radii to balance easy tap-target affordance with structural button clarity.

## Components

### Primary Conversion Buttons
- **Terracotta CTA:** Background `#D95B1E`, text `#FFFFFF`, bold `label-lg`, rounded `0.75rem`. Minimum tap target `52px` height on mobile. Subtle warm shadow `rgba(217, 91, 30, 0.25)`.
- **Secondary Booking / Inquire:** Border `2px solid #5B6440`, text `#5B6440`, transparent background. On hover: fills with light sage `#EEF1E6`.
- **Call-Now Emergency Button:** Secondary pill button with active telephone icon, styled in `#5B6440` with white text.

### Trust Proof & Review Badges
- **Google Star Pill:** `#FAF9F6` or `#FDF4DF` surface, featuring 5 `#F7C153` stars, `label-md` bold score ("5.0 Stars - 280+ Parent Reviews"), enclosed in a soft pill with a `#F7C153` hairline border.
- **Doctor Authority Badge:** Micro-avatar of the board-certified pediatric dentist combined with verified badge iconography and clear credentials ("Board-Certified Pediatric Specialist").

### Form Fields & Instant Booking Inputs
- **Base Style:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #E5E7DC`, radius `0.75rem`, padding `0.875rem 1rem`, text `#222222`.
- **Focused State:** Border changes to `#5B6440` with an ambient glow of `0 0 0 3px rgba(91, 100, 64, 0.15)`.

### Feature & Comfort Cards
- **Structure:** Surface `#FFFFFF`, rounded `1.25rem`, padding `space-xl`, border `1px solid #E5E7DC`.
- **Icon Container:** Circular or squircle background in `#EEF1E6` housing forest green `#5B6440` iconography (e.g., sensory-friendly rooms, laser cavity care, parent-accompanied visits).

### Checkboxes & Segmented Age Selectors
- **Age Selectors:** Segmented pill toggles with radio semantics. Default state `#FAF9F6` with subtle border; selected state background `#5B6440`, text `#FFFFFF`, shadow `0 2px 8px rgba(91, 100, 64, 0.2)`.
