Restorative Pediatric Dentistry

Cavities, damaged teeth, and dental infections can make eating, sleeping, and everyday activities uncomfortable for a child. Our restorative treatments are planned around the condition of the tooth, your child’s age, and their individual comfort needs. Depending on your child’s needs, treatment may include pediatric dental fillings, crowns, pulpotomies, space maintainers, or tooth extractions.

Pediatric Sedation Dentistry

Some children need additional support to feel comfortable during dental treatment. Pediatric sedation may be considered for children who are anxious, have difficulty sitting through a procedure, require more involved treatment, or have individual behavioral or sensory needs.

Meet Dr. Amy | Board-Certified Pediatric Dentist

Dr. Amy JeeHye Huh is the founder and board-certified pediatric dentist at Little Forest Pediatric Dentistry. Her approach combines specialized pediatric training with a genuine focus on helping children feel understood, involved, and comfortable during dental visits.

Dr. Amy earned her Bachelor of Science in Molecular, Cell, and Developmental Biology from UCLA before receiving her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Western University of Health Sciences College of Dental Medicine.

She later completed her specialty training in pediatric dentistry at Loma Linda University and also serves as a part-time faculty member.

For Dr. Amy, pediatric dentistry is about more than treating teeth. She works closely with children and parents to encourage healthy habits, explain treatment clearly, and build confidence around dental care from an early age.

“I want to foster a kid-friendly, welcoming space for my patients and their families. My goal is to promote good oral health while making each pediatric dental visit enjoyable and informative.” — Dr. Amy

Children should generally have their first dental visit when their first tooth appears or by their first birthday. Starting early allows the dentist to monitor oral development, provide preventive guidance, and help your child become familiar with dental visits.
