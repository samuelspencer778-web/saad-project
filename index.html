Helpful Information

Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions before your visit? Here are the most common questions our patient concierge receives.

How much is the Lumina Dentistry new patient special? expand_more
Our new patient visit is designed to make getting started with dental care simple and affordable. The $129 visit includes a comprehensive exam, digital X-rays, professional cleaning, and complimentary whitening for eligible new patients. Our team will review your dental needs and explain any recommended treatment options clearly.
Do you accept patients without dental insurance? expand_more
Yes, Lumina Dentistry welcomes patients with or without dental insurance. We offer flexible payment options and membership solutions to help make dental care more accessible. Our team will discuss available options with you so you can choose a payment approach that works best for your needs.
Can I schedule a same-day dental appointment? expand_more
If you are experiencing tooth pain or an urgent dental concern, our team may be able to provide a same-day appointment based on availability. We understand that dental problems can be stressful, so we work to provide timely care and help you understand your treatment options as quickly as possible.
Is Lumina Dentistry accepting new patients? expand_more
Yes, Lumina Dentistry is currently welcoming new patients of all ages. Whether you need a routine cleaning, have dental concerns, or are looking for a new dental home, our team provides personalized care in a comfortable and welcoming environment.
What can I expect during my first visit? expand_more
During your first visit, our team will take time to understand your dental history, evaluate your oral health, and discuss your goals. We focus on making the experience comfortable by explaining each step clearly and answering any questions before recommending treatment.
Does Lumina Dentistry provide care for children and adults? expand_more
Yes, Lumina Dentistry provides dental care for both children and adults. Our team focuses on creating a comfortable experience for every patient, from a child’s first dental visit to ongoing preventive and restorative care for adults.
